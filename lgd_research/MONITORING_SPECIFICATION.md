# Research monitoring specification

At each observation date capture model version, scoring timestamp, facility/customer ID, input provenance and availability, collateral/guarantee evidence, PD/stage/EAD, LGD forecast, exposure and eventual recovery-vintage ID. Do not compare forecasts to unresolved defaults as if their final recoveries were known; report resolution coverage and lag. Preserve one immutable scored snapshot per date.

Monthly population controls: count/EAD, zero-guarantee share, security/guarantor missingness, collateral mix, industry/product/lien mix, numeric input quantiles, unseen categories and out-of-range inputs, forecast distribution, and drift against the *approved* model's development population. These are warning diagnostics; thresholds must be bank-approved before automation. A new unsupported category or missing required feature blocks scoring rather than silently imputing an invented guarantee.

Quarterly, when enough workouts resolve: overall and EAD-weighted MAE/RMSE/bias, predicted-decile calibration, fixed *forecast-selected* top-risk cohorts, realized >60/>75/>90 cohorts with N and EAD, cure and non-cure outcomes, recovery component and timing validation, and bootstrap intervals. A small number of near-total losses must be flagged as low precision. Compare consecutive vintages and underwriting changes; preserve the identity between model forecasts and booked allowances, including any separately approved overlay.

**Escalation:** structural identity failure, absent input provenance or leakage blocks use immediately. Material persistent bias, support drift, adverse stress response or a control-limit breach triggers independent validation and risk-governance review. No numerical amber/red threshold is set by synthetic results; define quantitative limits using real-bank loss experience, accounting materiality and approved risk appetite. Keep V5 bank-use gate blocked until institution-specific validation, live feature availability and approval records exist.
