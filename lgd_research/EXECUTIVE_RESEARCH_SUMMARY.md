# LGD research decision — 24 September 2026

**Outcome G: further redevelopment required.** Retain published V5 (`0dfe4c8`) as the educational incumbent, do not promote an experimental LGD model, and keep the bank-use gate blocked. This is an isolated research branch; no method was merged into the governed V5 source.

The existing V5 2,000-workout holdout has near-neutral whole-population bias (−0.31 percentage points) but underpredicts 327 **realized** losses >75% by 15.59 points. The positive error in low-loss/cure outcomes offsets much of the negative error in high-loss/non-cure outcomes. Only one of 6,000 original development workouts has zero guarantee coverage, whereas 4,096 of 5,172 active synthetic facilities do; the source data do not support the dominant current input combination.

The research generated a separate recovery process with correlated stress, collateral and guarantor quality, stochastic cures and dated net cash flows. The independent final holdout was frozen by SHA before candidate selection and contains **3,000 defaults**, including **536 realized LGDs >75%** with **295.47m synthetic EAD** out of **1,669.40m**. On that process the H-trained V5 model has MAE **12.77 pp**, RMSE **17.65 pp**, mean bias **−0.82 pp**, and realized-severe bias **−12.74 pp**. The enhanced two-stage challenger has MAE **11.13 pp**, RMSE **15.69 pp**, bias **+0.22 pp**, and realized-severe bias **−12.05 pp**. The paired overall RMSE difference (challenger minus V5) has synthetic iid bootstrap 95% interval **[−2.27, −1.63] pp**; the severe-bias difference interval **[−0.18, +1.56] pp** includes zero. The severe outcome problem persists.

The non-deployable oracle with future cure, shocks and resolution timing attains MAE **4.05 pp** on the same final sample. This strongly supports an information-gap explanation *within the synthetic process*. R2's new security quality, guarantor strength and downturn features are not present in the live V5 input data; the oracle's future fields can never legitimately be used at decision time. More elaborate models cannot turn these synthetic results into an institution's empirical evidence.

The [methodology log](LGD_METHODOLOGY_CHANGELOG.md), [experiment register](LGD_EXPERIMENT_REGISTER.md), [findings](LGD_FINDINGS_REGISTER.md), [data generation register](LGD_DATA_GENERATION_REGISTER.md) and [reproduction instructions](REPRODUCIBILITY.md) preserve failed tests and boundaries.

**Next evidence needed before any bank use:** actual resolved recoveries with dated costs, independent legal collateral and guarantee information at the score timestamp, sufficiently long economic vintages, a prospective validation sample, approval of a particular expected-LGD methodology, and a separate ECL impact review. The research programme does not alter live allowances or release the production gate.
