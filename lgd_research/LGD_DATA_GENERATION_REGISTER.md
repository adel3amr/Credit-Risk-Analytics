**R2 assumptions before results:** product mix .42 term/.25 overdraft/.12 LC/.13 performance guarantee/.08 financial guarantee; collateral mix .36 none/.10 cash/.34 mortgage/.20 other. Collateral values vary by type. Original values remain separate. Predefault observable `security_quality` and `guarantor_strength` are imperfect proxies. Downturn is sampled ex ante with prevalence .22 and correlates collateral realization, guarantee collection, unsecured recoveries, costs and delay. Cure probability is stochastic conditional on borrower health, downturn and security; no model may use realized cure. Discounts apply to recovery and cost flows. Explicit stochastic shocks remain even after observing all legal predictors; oracle alone may see postdefault outcomes. H/R1 and R2 are different target processes: cross-generator score comparisons reveal portability, **not like-for-like historical improvement**.

**Freeze rule:** no generator edit or seed change based on final-holdout scores. If a defect is found after final evaluation, record it and require a new version and a genuinely new holdout. The R2 final holdout is a synthetic independent seed, **not** an actual time split or real bank test.
