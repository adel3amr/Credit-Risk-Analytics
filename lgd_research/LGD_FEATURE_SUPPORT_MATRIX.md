The most important support failure is H's missing exact zero guarantee; R2 improves that region but its 60% still differs materially from the active portfolio's 79.2%. R2's `security_quality`, `guarantor_strength` and `downturn_at_default` are observable by construction *in the experimental generator at default*, but are absent in the active V5 source. An enhanced challenger using them **cannot be deployed on V5's current inputs**; treating zero as their value would not be legitimate missing-data handling. V5 also forecasts facilities at reporting date whereas the training information is drawn at default: actual production transfer requires a time-aligned, institution-specific definition.

Product, industry, collateral type and lien categories are present in H, R2 and live; their exact counts appear in the CSV. Numeric range comparisons, missingness, zeros and quantiles appear there too. These are descriptive and do not constitute a formal approval for extrapolation or equality of the recovery process.
