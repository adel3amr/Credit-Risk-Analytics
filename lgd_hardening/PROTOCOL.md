# Post-R1 hardening — preflight protocol, 28 September 2026

Baseline: `14b8c4db691c8f795c27501b7237442426eb70b2`. Preserve V5, the tail
programme, S1, S2 and R1. R1 promotion outcomes are now **known engineering
evidence**, never a fresh holdout. No final population will be generated/opened
until all material engineering and data-contract issues have been resolved.

First reproduce hashes, tests and material metrics. Audit the four failures
separately: domain mismatch; uncertainty with only seven current economic
clusters; guarantee composition; high prediction-band expected-loss errors.
Trace imported hidden qualities back to their source rather than assuming S2's
exclusion of a direct state column removed all state-dependent pathways.

One focused calibration correction may be assessed: continuous monotone
calibration of the frozen R1 prediction, fitted exclusively to the 6,000 matured
R1 calibration **realized** baseline workouts. Twenty equal-count ordered bins
(approximately 300 each), weighted isotonic pooling and linear interpolation
between learned knots. No band-specific offsets, hidden features, conditional
truth targets, current outcomes or old promotion outcomes enter fitting. No
hyperparameter/seed search. Flat tails are explicitly disclosed; both original
and recalibrated prediction bands are reported. Reject if engineering strengths
are lost. Any other candidate requires a diagnosed new cause and version.

Support controls will distinguish the existing DGP mechanical domain from the
empirically supported model domain. The mechanical bounds and scenario deltas
remain those already governed in S2, not bounds chosen around failed rows.
No silent scenario clipping. Engineering comparisons may retain diagnostic raw
predictions, but operational output requires support and promotion. OOD treatment
is rejection, with an explicitly non-bookable [0,1] LGD sensitivity bound.

Do not make unavailable guarantor/security quality operational by copying a
simulator column into a new contract. A future capture contract must identify
dated source evidence and a separately validated feature derivation. In its
absence, report the feature/deployability blocker rather than fabricate data.

Numerical acceptance tolerances remain R1's 1 pp aggregate/regime and 2 pp
material-segment equivalence bounds; zero unexplained reversals; unchanged ECL
arithmetic tolerances. Do not manufacture precision by duplicating borrowers,
quarters or Monte Carlo draws as independent economic histories. Sample-size
planning must identify the independent unit and distinguish bias from variance.

If engineering passes, freeze model/calibration, domain/DGP, validation design,
sampling weights and power before generating an independent promotion population.
If a genuine unavailable-information/unsupported-assumption blocker remains,
do not consume a new holdout or label engineering results final validation.

Classifications: VALIDATION (estimand, uncertainty, power, band audit), MODEL
IMPLEMENTATION/METHODOLOGY (versioned monotone calibration), GOVERNANCE/DATA
ARCHITECTURE (domain and capture contracts), BUG FIX (any demonstrated release
or scenario-control defect). No PD/EAD/staging/SICR/EWS changes are authorized by
this focused implementation. Bank-use approval remains a separate gate.
