## EAD / ECL / scenario contracts

EAD consumes drawn, limit, product and face at reporting date: term uses drawn; OVD uses limit; trade uses face × frozen CCF (.20/.50/1), rounded to cents. Missing and negative amounts reject. ECL consumes governed forward PD, LGD, EAD, assigned stage and remaining months. PD/LGD must be finite fractions, stage must be one of three values, maturity nonnegative. Stage 2 retains constant hazard on weighted PD; it is not a new scenario-lifetime methodology. Scenarios require exactly one baseline, unique names, finite values and nonnegative weights summing to one.

Institution-specific refresh SLAs, data ownership, guarantee enforceability and measurement definitions remain capture/qualification requirements. Future data may be used for outcome validation, never scoring.
