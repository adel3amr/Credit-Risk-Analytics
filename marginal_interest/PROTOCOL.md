## Question and retrospective feasibility
Does suspended contractual interest known at observation add information about remaining workout LGD beyond age, balance, rate, borrower risk and recovery state? Existing S2-R1 generation supplies static facilities, rate, hypothetical reporting dates and final recovery buckets. It does not maintain a dated suspended-interest ledger, capitalization policy or payment allocation history. Historical reporting dates are not validated impairment dates. Therefore retrospective MI cannot be reconstructed legitimately. No historical outcomes will be used or relabelled.

## Accounting and target
MI means a noncapitalized memorandum balance of contractual interest accrued monthly on opening principal since impairment, with no compounding, interest payments or fees in this explicit hypothetical policy. Principal payments reduce future accrual. This is NOT IFRS interest revenue, a booked receivable, or a universal Egyptian policy. MI is only a predictor; observation EAD is remaining principal. It is never added to EAD or loss. Remaining LGD = clip(1 - PV(future net principal recoveries)/observation principal,0,1). Past principal recoveries are excluded from future cashflows; target is a new in-default observation-date target, NOT a silent replacement for historical default-date LGD. All observations are impaired Stage 3 at observation; later cure is outcome only. Stage1/2 ECL changes are not applicable and must not be fabricated.

Hypothetical temporal split, not empirical backtest. Source macro/features are inherited known covariates, new dates not historical macro claims. Keep natural sampled collateral/guarantee/product distribution without balancing.

Monthly principal payment probability sigmoid(-1.5 + willingness + .4*quarter_shock); positive amount opening balance*Uniform(.005,.045). Willingness Normal(0,1), quarter shock Normal(0,.4). Interest accrues opening balance*contractual_rate/12 before principal payment. Restructuring known at observation Bernoulli(sigmoid(-1+.3*willingness)); no interest capitalization. Future recovery uses the existing economic recovery simulator with latent willingness affecting future current-ratio recovery capacity by .6*willingness (floor .05), and quarter shock by .2*shock. This is a hypothetical mechanism, not an estimated institutional coefficient. Additional cure probability sigmoid(-2+.5*willingness+.4*restructured) settles residual principal at month6. Net future costs remain. Frozen actual draw plus 128 separate Monte Carlo draws provide diagnostic conditional mean given simulator latent state; this oracle is not a model input/target and is NOT the observable-information conditional expectation. Evaluate calibration on realized outcomes as primary. No favourable-data regeneration.
